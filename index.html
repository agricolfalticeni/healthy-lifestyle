Who we are

Healthy Lifestyle Company is about the precious gifts that nature offers to us, the fascinating human connection with it, and the magic bond between people and nature.

This company was founded to promote a natural, healthy, and balanced diet, so that people always feel connected to nature, its richness, and its beauties.

The business idea

From the forests of Bucovina, to the forefront.

Healthy Lifestyle Company is a Romanian business with exclusive private ownership. Our main activities include collecting, processing, and distributing organic forest fruit and medicinal plants.

Point zero 2023

2023 marks the beginning of our journey into the world of wild plants from Bucovina. We aim to bring the natural riches of this region to the forefront.
